========== FO5 [officer, role=kahramaa, en] Why are wells in the north getting saltier?
20:25:35 FAST      explain (0.88), 1 fact(s), answered in 4.4 s
---------- 4.4 s | path=fast explain | persona=officer | simplified=False | language_ok=True
ANSWER:
Pumping groundwater for farms faster than rain refills it lets seawater move inland; in the north the saline front has been advancing about 4-5 km per year [Elobaid_2017_EGU_saline_front_Qatar.txt p.2]. This means that wells in the north are increasingly exposed to seawater intrusion. Monitoring should focus on areas near the advancing saline front to manage salinity levels effectively.
