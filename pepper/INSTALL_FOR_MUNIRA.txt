AquaMaa on Pepper: install the update (about 2 minutes)
=======================================================

WHAT'S NEW IN THIS VERSION
- Pepper's tablet shows the live AquaNova dashboard over the full screen, with a caption strip at the bottom:
  what Pepper heard, and what it is saying (answers and alerts).
- Pepper answers questions with AquaMaa's AI and the live sensor data, and speaks alerts by itself.
- It finds Roaa's laptop on the Wi-Fi by itself, even if the laptop's address changes. No more rebuilds for that.
- The website's chat box is hidden on Pepper: on the robot, people talk to Pepper itself.

ADDRESSES
- Wi-Fi for everything: Pepper's router (HUAWEI-89AB / HUAWEI-8983, same router, 192.168.8.x)
- Pepper's tablet (Run/Debug Config): 192.168.8.101
- Roaa's laptop (AquaMaa + dashboard): 192.168.8.109 right now. The app finds it anyway if this changes.

INSTALL (from your laptop, the one the tablet already trusts)
1. Your laptop on Pepper's Wi-Fi.
2. On Pepper's tablet: Settings -> Developer options -> ADB ON.
3. In a terminal (or Android Studio's Terminal), in the folder with AquaMaa_Pepper.apk:
       adb connect 192.168.8.101:5555
       adb install -r AquaMaa_Pepper.apk
   If it says INSTALL_FAILED_UPDATE_INCOMPATIBLE (the old app was signed on another laptop):
       adb uninstall com.example.pepperwater
       adb install AquaMaa_Pepper.apk
   ("adb" is in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Android\Sdk\platform-tools\ if it isn't on your PATH.)

START IT
4. On the tablet: ADB OFF. Wait for the notification "Connected to Pepper".
   (With ADB on, the app can't use the robot: Pepper can't listen or speak.)
5. Open the AquaMaa app. Pepper says: "Hello! I am AquaMaa. I watch the wells..."
6. The dashboard appears. Sign in once on the tablet:  officer@aquamaa.demo  /  AquaMaa-Demo-2026
7. The yellow text at the bottom right should say: "Listening... ask me about the water".

TALK TO PEPPER (English, about 1 m in front, after its eyes turn blue)
   "hello" . "what is the salinity now" . "is it good for irrigation" . "is the water safe to drink"
   "what does the dashboard show" . "give me the briefing" . "why are the wells getting saltier"
   Pepper says a short line ("Let me check the sensor"), then AquaMaa's answer (5-10 s).

IF SOMETHING IS WRONG (read the yellow text at the bottom right)
- "Starting..."                         -> ADB is still on, or the tablet isn't "Connected to Pepper" yet.
- "Looking for the AquaMaa laptop..."   -> wait a few seconds; Roaa's laptop must be on the same Wi-Fi with
                                           AquaMaa running.
- "AquaMaa laptop not found..."         -> tap the yellow text and type Roaa's laptop address (192.168.8.x).
- "Robot focus refused"                 -> close other apps on the tablet, open AquaMaa again.
- A blank page instead of the dashboard -> after 12 s it switches to AquaMaa's simple live page by itself.
