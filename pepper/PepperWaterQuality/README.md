# Pepper Water Quality - Hello World (Pepper 1.8, NAOqi 2.9, QiSDK)

Pepper greets you and answers spoken questions about water quality.

* Questions & answers: app/src/main/res/raw/water_quality.top
* Robot logic:         app/src/main/java/com/example/pepperwater/MainActivity.kt
* Tablet screen:       app/src/main/res/layout/activity_main.xml

Build + install: set PEPPER_IP in build_and_run.bat, turn ADB ON on the tablet,
double-click build_and_run.bat, then turn ADB OFF and open the app on the tablet.
See the PDF guide for full instructions.
