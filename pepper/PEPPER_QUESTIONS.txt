QUESTIONS TO ASK PEPPER (for the demo and for testing)
=====================================================
Pepper's app version: the zip from 4:56 PM or later. Check: the yellow text at the bottom right says
"Listening (keywords)...". Speak in English, about 1 m in front of Pepper, after its eyes turn blue, one short
sentence at a time. Pepper says a short line at once ("Let me check the sensor"), then AquaMaa's answer (5-15 s).
The answers are never written in advance: they come live from the sensor, the dashboard and AquaMaa's sources.

THE DEMO, IN ORDER (best questions)
1. Hello Pepper.                                        -> greeting
2. What is the salinity now?                            -> the current salinity and what it means
3. Is the water safe to drink?                          -> drinking, from the live reading
4. Can farmers use this water for date palms?           -> irrigation and which crops tolerate it
5. What is the pH of the water?                         -> pH now and what it means
6. Are there any problems with the wells?               -> which sensors need attention or are offline
7. Thank you, Pepper.                                   -> goodbye

MORE QUESTIONS THAT WORK (each has a keyword Pepper listens for, in CAPITALS)
Salinity:      How SALTY is the WATER today? . What is the SALINITY? . What does the SENSOR say?
Temperature:   What is the water TEMPERATURE? . Is the water HOT?
pH:            Is the water ACIDIC? . What is the PH?
All readings:  What are the current READINGS? . Tell me the STATUS.
Drinking:      Can people DRINK this water? . Is it DRINKABLE?
Irrigation:    Is it good for IRRIGATION? . Can I water my PALMS? . Is it OK for my FARM? . What about TOMATOES?
Trend:         Is the water getting SALTIER? . Is it getting WORSE? . Is salinity RISING?
Why:           WHY are the wells getting salty?
Alerts:        Any ALERTS? . Any PROBLEMS? . Give me the BRIEFING. . Which sensors are OFFLINE?
Dashboard:     What does the DASHBOARD show? . What is on the SCREEN?
Law:           Do farmers need a LICENCE? . What is the FINE for an illegal well? . Is a PERMIT needed?

KEYWORDS PEPPER LISTENS FOR (any sentence with one of them works)
greeting:     hello, hi, hey, good morning, salam
salinity:     salt, salty, salinity, TDS, quality, reading, sensor, water
temperature:  temperature, hot, cold, warm, degrees
pH:           pH, acid, acidic, acidity, alkaline
all readings: readings, values, everything, status, situation
drinking:     drink, drinking, drinkable, potable
irrigation:   irrigate, irrigation, crops, palms, dates, farm, farming, tomatoes, plants
trend:        saltier, trend, rising, increasing, worse, changing
why:          why, reason, cause
alerts:       alert, problem, wrong, attention, offline, briefing
dashboard:    dashboard, screen, page, show
law:          licence, license, permit, law, fine, penalty, legal
goodbye:      thanks, thank you, bye, goodbye

TIPS
- If the strip says "Heard something, but did not understand", the sentence had no keyword: try one from the list.
- If the yellow text says "Starting...", ADB is still on: switch ADB OFF and reopen the app.
- With the sensor offline, Pepper honestly says there is no current reading and gives the last known value.
  For the demo, Munira's sensor must be sending.
- Alerts (salty water, pH too high or low, sensor offline for 30 min) are spoken by Pepper without being asked.
